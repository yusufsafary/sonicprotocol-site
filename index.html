FAQ
Questions

    

      
I don't have an Agentic Wallet. Can I join?
You can't mint, but you can buy cards on Element, keep them, trade them and fuse them right here in My Deck with any wallet.

      
How do I get an Agent ID?
Open the Binance Wallet app, set up an Agentic Wallet and claim your .ai Agent ID from the banner. Your agent can check with baw wallet agent-id.

      
How many Tones make a Sonic Boom?
27. Three Tones make a Chord, three Chords make a Harmony, three Harmonies make a Sonic Boom. One Agent ID can mint 11 cards at most, so a Sonic Boom always takes trading or teamwork.

      
Is the art on-chain?
Traits, seeds and fusion history are on-chain. The card images are rendered from that data by our open server code and served from sonicprotocol.fun.

      
Why can't I hear anything?
Phones block sound until you tap. Tap a card once, and check your silent switch.
